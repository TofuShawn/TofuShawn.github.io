**One.** The quietest interface I use every day has no sound at all. That is not
an argument against sound; it is an argument for it being *off* by default and
easy to turn on.

**Two.** A page that plays a tone on load has made a decision on my behalf about
whether I am in a meeting. A page that plays a tone when I click a seal has not.

**Three.** Reading is a rhythm. Anything that interrupts the rhythm — a popup, a
sound, a layout shift — costs more than it delivers, and the cost is paid in
attention that does not come back.

**Four.** The best compliment I have received about this site is that someone
forgot it was a website. That is the whole ambition: to be a document that happens
to be served over HTTP.
